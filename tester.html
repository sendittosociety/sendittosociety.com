⚠ Windows is going to try to stop you

Windows protected your PC Microsoft Defender SmartScreen prevented an unrecognized app from starting.

Click "More info", then "Run anyway." That's the whole fix.

This isn't a virus warning. It's a "we don't know who wrote this" warning, and it appears on every app that hasn't paid for a code-signing certificate. That's about $120 a year and it's on my list, behind things that matter more right now. I'd rather tell you straight than have you find it on your own and quietly decide not to open it.

Installing

  1. Download it from sendittosociety.com/download

    About 212 MB. That is the whole app, including the offline transcription model — nothing else gets fetched later.

  2. Run it. Get past the Windows warning above.

  3. The installer asks who to install for. "Only for me" is already selected — just click Install. Nothing else to decide.

  4. It opens on a black EARLY ACCESS screen. Press Enter, or click anywhere.

It's going to look empty

This is the part I most want you to expect, because otherwise it reads as broken.

You're starting a channel from nothing, so the app has nothing to show you yet. The 3D city will be a mostly empty lot. Your stats will be zeroes. Your rank will be blank. All of that is correct. The city grows as you actually upload, and you haven't uploaded.

And on that — the city, the ⚡ Buzz, the ranks. It looks like a game and it half is one, but it's not decoration. Everything in it is driven by real numbers from your real channel. It's there because a spreadsheet has never once made anybody want to open it on a Tuesday.

The fourteen days

It runs in full for fourteen days from the first time you open it — every tool, every channel, the city, the games. No card, no account, nothing to sign up for.

On day fifteen it stops and asks for a licence key. Nothing you made is deleted — every script, every card, every building is still there and comes straight back the moment a key goes in. If you're still testing when you hit it, just tell me and I'll send you a key; don't let it stop you.

Say something if that wall feels unfair when you hit it. You're the only person who'll ever see it fresh.

The AI features

You don't need an API key and you don't need a paid subscription. The default mode is free and works like this:

  • The app writes the prompt for you
  • You paste it into whatever AI you already use — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, your pick in Settings → AI
  • You paste the answer back

That's it. If you'd rather it was one click and you already have an API key, there's a One-Click mode in the same place.

Honest warnings

  • It's a beta. Things will break. That's the point of you being here.
  • Your data is yours and it's local. It lives in your AppData folder, not on a server. I can't see any of it.
  • Nothing leaves your machine unless you deliberately use a feature that goes out to the internet — searching for footage, or an AI feature.
  • It won't update itself. When I fix something I'll send you a new installer.

If you want it gone

Windows Settings → Apps → Send It To Society → Uninstall. Your projects stay in AppData afterwards in case you change your mind, and you can delete that folder yourself.
