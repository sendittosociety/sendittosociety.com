// EVERY TOOL, AND WHAT IT ACTUALLY DOES

    
Under the hood

    
Fourteen tools. What each one is, how you'd use it, and
      how it works underneath. Open any of them — real screens, not mockups.


    

      
    


    

    

      
        
PRODUCTION PIPELINE

        Seven stages, and every video is a card moving through them.
      
      

        
        

          
WHAT IT IS
A board where every video is a card, and every card moves through seven stages: idea, research, scripting, record, gather, editing, done.

          
HOW YOU’D USE IT
One look tells you what is written but not filmed, what is filmed but not cut, and what has been sitting in research for three weeks.

          
UNDER THE HOOD
Rename any stage, reorder them, hide the ones your workflow does not use — except idea and done, which can never be hidden, because you always need somewhere to start and somewhere to finish. Each card carries its own notes, script, packaging and files, so a video’s whole history travels with it instead of scattering across five apps.

        

      

    

    

      
        
SCRIPT LAB · THE VERDICT

        Your next video graded before you post it.
      
      

        
        

          
WHAT IT IS
A script goes in and comes back with a grade, a full breakdown, and exactly what to change — graded as short-form or long-form, because they are not the same craft.

          
HOW YOU’D USE IT
Paste the script, read the top fixes, do them, then post.

          
UNDER THE HOOD
Five categories — concept, packaging, hook, retention and pacing, satisfaction and engagement — combined into a 0–100 package score and a letter grade, then multiplied by an originality factor. A compliance gate can block a score outright rather than quietly averaging the problem away. The craft cheat sheet is on the same page: the beats it grades against, the title patterns that convert, and the three laws — net information gain, good versus bad abandonment, and the closer as the rewatch.

        

      

    

    

      
        
HOOK PRINTER

        The first ten seconds, printed and graded F through S.
      
      

        
        

          
WHAT IT IS
Eight hook recipes — The Question, Bold Claim, Visual Reveal, The Comparison, The Mystery, Stat Drop, Story Open, Pattern Break — each a blueprint with slots to fill.

          
HOW YOU’D USE IT
Pick a recipe, fill its slots, print, read why it scored what it scored, and print again. The tenth hook is better than the first because you were told what was wrong with the other nine.

          
UNDER THE HOOD
S needs a score of 92 or better, and the app calls it a PLASMA-CUT MASTER PRINT when you get one. Recipes are structured slots rather than blank prompts, so a hook is composed rather than guessed at.

        

      

    

    

      
        
TRANSCRIBE

        Talk it out loud; get the script back, on your own machine.
      
      

        
        

          
WHAT IT IS
Drop a voiceover or a recording and get a script back — fast, balanced or accurate, your call on the trade.

          
HOW YOU’D USE IT
Say the video out loud, then edit the transcript into the script instead of writing from a blank page.

          
UNDER THE HOOD
The speech model runs on your PC — no API key, no per-minute fee, and the audio never leaves the machine. The result is shaped into Hook / Body / CTA and can be sent straight into a new card on the pipeline.

        

      

    

    

      
        
THUMB FORGE

        A real layer-based thumbnail editor, not a template filler.
      
      

        
        

          
WHAT IT IS
A full thumbnail studio living next to the video it belongs to, with the layer stack, transform handles, rulers and guides you would expect from a desktop editor.

          
HOW YOU’D USE IT
Build two versions, flip between A and B, ship the one that wins — and check it against the shelf preview, which shows it at the size it will actually be seen at.

          
UNDER THE HOOD
Layers with blend modes, masks and clipping, groups, adjustments, arc text, free transform with rotation and crop, brush and selection tools, snapping, rulers, guides, a safe-area overlay, autosave and recovery. The rendering engine is separated from the interface and tested against reference images, which is why the export matches what you saw.

        

      

    

    

      
        
SEO LAB

        Script in, the whole upload package out.
      
      

        
        

          
WHAT IT IS
Your script and your niche go in; three title options, a paste-ready description, hashtags and a tag list come out.

          
HOW YOU’D USE IT
Paste the script, drop in up to ten videos that already worked in your lane, then copy the package straight into YouTube Studio.

          
UNDER THE HOOD
Those ten reference slots pull the real packaging off the videos you point at — their actual title, description and tag list — so what comes back is anchored to things that shipped and ranked. Honest packaging is enforced: every title it offers has to be kept by the script. If one feels like a stretch, it is — pick another.

        

      

    

    

      
        
THE ARCHIVE

        A dozen public-domain libraries in one search, cleared before you see it.
      
      

        
        

          
WHAT IT IS
One search across the Internet Archive, the Library of Congress, NASA, Wikimedia, Pexels and Pixabay.

          
HOW YOU’D USE IT
Search a mood, download the clip, drop it in the edit. The licence was settled before you ever saw the thumbnail.

          
UNDER THE HOOD
Clearance is keyed on membership of a cleared collection, not on the file’s own rights field — that field is empty on almost everything, so trusting it would clear nothing and block everything. Every download writes a receipt naming the licence it came under, and paths are validated before anything is written to disk.

        

      

    

    

      
        
THE SOUND BOOTH

        Free music and SFX, filtered by licence before you hear it.
      
      

        
        

          
WHAT IT IS
The Archive’s twin for sound, with three levels of strictness: CLEARED, FREE TO USE, or EVERYTHING — and it tells you what each one lets through.

          
HOW YOU’D USE IT
Find a bed or a hit, hear it in place, pick it, and keep the receipt with the project.

          
UNDER THE HOOD
Reaches Freesound, Jamendo, ccMixter and Incompetech through Openverse — no API key, direct MP3s. CC BY-SA is filtered out, and that one matters: cutting music to picture is legally an adaptation, so a BY-SA track would force your finished video to be BY-SA as well. NC and ND are never requested at all.

        

      

    

    

      
        
EVOLVE · OUTLIERS

        The videos beating their own channel’s average.
      
      

        
        

          
WHAT IT IS
The breakout videos across the channels you have chosen to track.

          
HOW YOU’D USE IT
Find the move that worked on a channel your size, then hit FORGE FROM THIS to start one of your own from it.

          
UNDER THE HOOD
Ranked by multiple of the channel’s own baseline, not by raw views — a 51× outlier on a small channel counts the same as one on a large channel. That is deliberate: you are copying the move, not the subscriber count that made it land.

        

      

    

    

      
        
EVOLVE · TRENDS

        What is actually pulling views in your niche, with the numbers.
      
      

        
        

          
WHAT IT IS
The words and formats moving views across the channels you watch.

          
HOW YOU’D USE IT
Check it before you title anything.

          
UNDER THE HOOD
Measured across the channels you track rather than across YouTube at large, so it is your corner of the platform and not general advice. ALL-CAPS emphasis at +499% versus typical. Question hooks at +85%. Listicles at −25%. The numbers move as your lane moves.

        

      

    

    

      
        
THE ACADEMY

        A school where the homework is done in the real tool.
      
      

        
        

          
WHAT IT IS
Four tracks — Content Creation 101, YouTube Growth, YouTube Automation and AI for Creators — taught by Sendy, with the Codex and the Playbook behind them as the reference library.

          
HOW YOU’D USE IT
Read a short lesson, then go and do the thing on your own channel. The app watches for it.

          
UNDER THE HOOD
Twenty lessons, each one READ → DO → QUIZ. The DO step is a real action detected in the real tool — a card created, a channel tracked, a hook printed — so a lesson cannot be completed by pressing next. Every wrong answer explains itself, and finishing one banks Buzz.

        

      

    

    

      
        
HEALTH

        The other half of the job: the person doing it.
      
      

        
        

          
WHAT IT IS
A wellness scheduler that lives in the same app as the work — recurring habits like training, meditation, reading and sleep, ticked off daily.

          
HOW YOU’D USE IT
Set the handful you actually intend to keep, then tick them the same way you tick a video off the pipeline.

          
UNDER THE HOOD
Discipline is measured rather than felt: a streak, a Vitality score, milestone badges, and a wellness district that grows in your city as you keep it up. It also feeds a capped bonus to your overall standing — capped on purpose, so nobody can out-meditate the fact that they haven’t shipped a video. Global: one body across every channel.

        

      

    

    

      
        
THE ARCADE

        Five games, because some days the honest move is to stop.
      
      

        
        

          
WHAT IT IS
SNAKE.EXE, BRICK BREAKER, SUB DEFENSE, SPACE DEFENSE and SOCIETY PINBALL, in a pixel cabinet with its own attract mode.

          
HOW YOU’D USE IT
On the day the script will not come, play something for ten minutes instead of staring at a blank page.

          
UNDER THE HOOD
Played for Buzz, the same currency everything else in the app costs. This is not a distraction bolted on: burnout is the real failure mode for someone running a channel alone, and an app with nowhere to go when you are stuck is an app you close.

        

      

    

    

      
        
CLOSED ON SUNDAYS

        The day the shop shuts.
      
      

        
        

          
WHAT IT IS
A weekly check-in for the person behind the channel. The Sunday Thought stays sealed until the shop actually closes.

          
HOW YOU’D USE IT
On Sunday it stops asking what you are shipping and asks how you are doing.

          
UNDER THE HOOD
A message for the day, the north star you are actually chasing, this week’s intentions, wins and gratitude, an honest read on life balance, and a quiet place to write. Work will still be there on Monday.
