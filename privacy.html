Privacy Policy

Send It To Society — last updated 1 September 2026

The short version

SITS runs on your computer. It has no accounts, no login, and no server of mine for it to talk to. I do not receive your scripts, your videos, your channel data, or anything else you do inside the app — not because I promise not to look, but because it is never sent anywhere.

Everything below is the longer version of that.

What the app stores, and where

All of your data lives on your own machine, in your Windows user folder (%APPDATA%). That includes your channels, your ideas and scripts, your production pipeline, your settings, and any API keys you choose to add.

I have no access to any of it. There is no sync, no cloud backup, and no telemetry. If you delete the app and its data folder, it is gone — I hold no copy.

When the app does reach the internet

SITS is not a walled garden, and some features work by fetching things. In every case the request goes to that service, not to me:

  • THE ARCHIVE / THE SOUND BOOTH — searching for footage and music sends your search terms to the sources being searched (Internet Archive, Openverse, Wikimedia Commons, NASA, the Library of Congress, Pexels, Pixabay, ccMixter and similar). Those services have their own privacy policies.
  • SEO LAB — fetching a video's public packaging sends a request to YouTube.
  • AI features — in the default copy-and-paste mode, nothing is sent anywhere: the app writes a prompt for you to paste into whatever AI you already use. If you add your own API key, requests go directly from your computer to that provider under your own account. They never pass through me.
  • Your own connected accounts — if you link a channel, that connection is between your computer and that platform, using credentials stored only on your machine.

The app does not check for updates or phone home on startup.

What I collect when you buy

Payment is handled by Paddle, who act as the merchant of record. They collect what a payment requires — your name, email address, billing country and payment details — and I never see your card details at any point.

From that transaction I receive and keep your name and email address, so that I can send you your licence key and support you afterwards. That is the entire customer record.

Your licence key contains the name it was issued to. That is how a leaked key can be traced back to the purchase it came from.

What I do with it

I use your email address to send your licence key, to answer you if you write to me, and to tell you about updates to the software you bought. Nothing else.

I do not sell your information, share it with advertisers, or add you to any marketing list you did not ask to be on.

How long I keep it

For as long as you have a licence, because that is what lets me help you if you lose your key. Ask me to delete your record and I will, and confirm when it is done. That means I can no longer reissue your key, so keep a copy of it first.

Your rights

You can ask me what I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email me and I will do it — no forms and no process, because there is one person here and the record is a name and an email address.

Depending on where you live you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA. Those rights apply, and the way to exercise them is the same: email me.

Children

SITS is not directed at children and I do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and the current version always lives at this address.
