Refund Policy

Send It To Society — last updated 1 September 2026

The whole policy

If SITS isn't what you expected, email me and I'll refund you. Same day, no questions, no form.

That is not a summary of something longer. That is the policy.

How to get one

Email sendittosociety@gmail.com from the address you bought with, and say you'd like a refund. You do not have to explain why, and I will not ask you to justify it or try to talk you out of it.

Refunds are processed through Paddle, who handled your payment. The money goes back to the card or account you paid with. Paddle usually completes it within a few business days, though how quickly it appears depends on your bank.

The time limit

30 days from purchase.

If you are outside that window and something has genuinely gone wrong — the app stopped working, it never ran on your machine, you were charged twice — write to me anyway. A date on a page is not a reason to keep money from someone who did not get what they paid for.

What happens to your licence

Your licence key stops being valid when a refund is issued, and the app stops running. Everything you made stays on your computer — your scripts, your pipeline, your projects are yours, and a refund does not touch a single file of it. If you ever come back, buy a key and it all opens up again exactly as you left it.

Why it works this way

SITS is a beta built by one person, it runs on Windows only, and Windows will show a security warning when you install it because I have not yet paid for a code-signing certificate. Those are real reasons to hesitate.

A refund policy with conditions attached would be asking you to take that risk on your own. This way the risk is mine, which is where it belongs.
